The Problem

There's a gap between what individuals learn and what organizations embed.

Countless day-to-day problems are dealt with — but not always systematically learned from.

01

Employees encounter problems that may recur across individuals, related to:

  • Project management
  • Operations
  • Customer service
  • Internal processes
02

Knowledge often remains siloed, including in employees' minds

Most of it does not spread consistently across the organization.

03

As a result:

  • Teams may at times repeat mistakes
  • Time may be spent rediscovering solutions to recurring problems
  • Valuable knowledge remains fragmented

And when employees eventually leave — the knowledge leaves with them.

04

Why this happens

How experiences are stored

① Not recorded ② Decentralized ③ Free text ④ Unstructured

Not broken down into problem, actions, and results (what worked and what didn't).

This makes them…

Difficult to search
Difficult to turn into insights
Ineffective for organizational learning
Organizations often lack an effective process to turn work experiences into learning — one that drives discipline and culture.
The Solution

WhatIDid — A platform designed to capture and structure work experiences

Using a simple, universal format that any employee can follow.

Work Experience =
Problem → Action → Result
(Worked, No Change, Got Worse)
🔴

Problem

Describe the operational problem or challenge faced in a clear, structured way.

⚡

Action

What was done to address the problem — the specific steps and decisions taken.

✅

Result

What happened — whether it worked, made no change, or made things worse.
